Addictions: Volume 2 is a Robert Palmer compilation album released in 1992. The album contained noted songs that Robert Palmer had from his Island Records albums "Riptide", "Sneakin' Sally Through the Alley", "Pressure Drop", "Double Fun", "Secrets", "Clues" and "Pride". The compilation album is known for most of its songs being either remixes, remakes or revoiced, with the exception of songs 5, 10, 14 and 15.
