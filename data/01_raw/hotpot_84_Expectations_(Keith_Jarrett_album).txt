Expectations is a 1972 album by the pianist, saxophonist and composer Keith Jarrett, released on Columbia Records. In addition to Jarrett, musicians on the recording include Dewey Redman (tenor saxophone), Charlie Haden (bass), Paul Motian (drums), Sam Brown (guitar), Airto Moreira (percussion), as well as brass and string sections whose members are not credited in the album information. "Expectations" was produced by George Avakian.
