Renhuai Maotai Airport (IATA: WMT) is an airport being constructed to serve the county-level city of Renhuai, which is under the administration of the prefecture-level city of Zunyi in Guizhou Province, China. It is located in Yinshui Village, Daping Township. The airport is named after the famous Maotai liquor produced in the nearby town of the same name. It will be the second airport in Zunyi, after Zunyi Xinzhou Airport.
