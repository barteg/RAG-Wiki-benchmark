A free-ranging dog is a dog that is not contained. Free-ranging dogs include wild dogs, feral dogs, stray dogs, street dogs, and village dogs, as well as dogs allowed to come and go freely by their owners. The term is used when distinctions of ownership are irrelevant. It sometimes overlaps with the polysemic term "pariah dog". It is estimated that free-ranging dogs make up 75–80% of the global dog population. The World Health Organization reports that there are 200 million stray dogs worldwide.
