Nanjing–Qidong Railway (), also known as the Ningqi Railway, is a railway in Jiangsu Province of China between the cities of Nanjing and Nantong. Its name is derived from "Ning" the shorthand name for Nanjing and Qidong, a district of Nantong. It is 268.3 km in length and runs along the northern bank of the lower Yangtze River. The planned easternmost section of the railway from Nantong to Qidong has not yet been built.
