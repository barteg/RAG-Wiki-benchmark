"After All" is a 1989 "Billboard" Hot 100 hit song performed as a duet by American singer and actress Cher and American singer and bass player Peter Cetera (former lead vocalist of Chicago), released on February 21, 1989, by Geffen Records. It was used as the love theme for the movie "Chances Are" and was nominated for Best Original Song at the Academy Awards 1989. The song was also the first North American single release from Cher's nineteenth album "Heart of Stone". The song appears on Peter Cetera's 1997 album "" and his 2017 album, "The Very Best of Peter Cetera".
