Christopher Eugene "Chris" O'Donnell (born June 26, 1970) is an American actor. He played Dick Grayson/Robin in "Batman Forever" and "Batman & Robin", Charlie Simms in "Scent of a Woman", Finn Dandridge in "Grey's Anatomy", Peter Garrett in "Vertical Limit", and Jack McAuliffe in "The Company". O'Donnell currently stars as NCIS Special Agent G. Callen on the CBS crime drama television series "", a spin-off of "NCIS".
