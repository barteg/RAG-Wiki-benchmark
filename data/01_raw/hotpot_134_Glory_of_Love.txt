"Glory of Love" is a 1986 song by Peter Cetera written and composed by Cetera, David Foster, and Cetera's then-wife Diane Nini, and recorded by Cetera shortly after he left the band Chicago to pursue a solo career. Featured in the 1986 film "The Karate Kid Part II", it was Cetera's first hit single after he left the team, reaching number one on the "Billboard" Hot 100, and it was included on his 1986 album, "Solitude/Solitaire", which Michael Omartian produced.<ref name="Solitude/SolitaireCD"> </ref> (Cetera released this album to coincide with the theatrical release of "The Karate Kid Part II".)
