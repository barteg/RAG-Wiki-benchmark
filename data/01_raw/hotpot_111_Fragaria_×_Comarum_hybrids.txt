There are several commercially important hybrids between "Fragaria" and "Comarum" species in existence, but they have not yet received formal botanical names as hybrids between those genera. Both "Fragaria" and "Comarum" have at different times been considered to be part of the genus "Potentilla", and these hybrids have been named Potentilla" x "rosea, or are sometimes referred to as "Fragaria" × "Potentilla" hybrids.
