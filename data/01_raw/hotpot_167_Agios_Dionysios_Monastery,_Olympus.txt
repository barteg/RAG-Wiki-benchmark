The Monastery of Agios Dionysios in Olympus (Greek: Μονή Αγίου Διονυσίου εν Ολύμπω ) is a Greek Orthodox monastery on the slopes of Mount Olympus, the most important monastery in the Pieria Prefecture. The original monastery, located at the Enipeas ravine at an altitude of 850 m and established in 1542, was destroyed by the Germans in 1943, and a new monastery was established near the town of Litochoro. Beside the monasteries Kanalon and Agia Triada, Sparmos it is one of the monasteries located at Mount Olympus.
