The Golden Jubilee Album is a compilation album by Australian band The Seekers. The album was released in November 2012, to celebrate 50 years since Judith Durham started her new job at an advertising agency in Melbourne and met account executive Athol Guy. Guy invited Durham to sit in with his mates Keith Potger and Bruce Woodley and perform in a little Melbourne coffee lounge called 'Treble Clef' on 3 December 1962. The quartet became known as The Seekers and released their first studio album in 1963, titled "Introducing the Seekers".
