Phreatichthys andruzzii is a species of ray-finned fish in the family Cyprinidae. It is the only species of the genus Phreatichthys, and is endemic to Somalia. This cave-adapted fish is whitish (non-pigmented) and blind. It is considered to have evolved in the cave environment for some two million years. Its name derives from the Greek words "phreasatos" for spring, and "ichthys" for fish. It grows to a maximum length of 6.2 cm .
