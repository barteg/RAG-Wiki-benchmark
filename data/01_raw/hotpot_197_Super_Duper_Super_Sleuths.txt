My Friends Tigger & Pooh: Super-Duper Super Sleuths is one of three "Winnie the Pooh" films based on the hit Playhouse Disney series "My Friends Tigger & Pooh". This is the fifth "Winnie the Pooh" film to feature Lumpy the Heffalump, and was released Straight-to-DVD on April 6, 2010. It aired on Playhouse Disney four days after on April 10, 2010. The film was produced by Walt Disney Television Animation, with animation production by Polygon Pictures in Japan.
