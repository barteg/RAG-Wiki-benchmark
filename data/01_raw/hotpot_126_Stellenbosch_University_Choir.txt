Stellenbosch University Choir (Afrikaans: "Stellenbosch Universiteitskoor" ) is a Choir attached to Stellenbosch University. Founded in 1936, it is the oldest choir in South Africa. The current conductor, André van der Merwe, was appointed at the beginning of 2003. The Stellenbosch University Choir has toured overseas extensively and won critical acclaim for its performances. The choir is viewed as a leading South African choral ensemble. In October 2016, the choir celebebrated its 80th anniversary with a reunion.
