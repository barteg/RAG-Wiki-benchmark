The Face of Medusa (Greek: Το πρόσωπο της Μέδουσας , translit. To prosopo tis Medousas and also known as "Vortex") is a 1967 Greek drama film directed by Nikos Koundouros. It was entered into the 17th Berlin International Film Festival. It features a beautiful man-eating woman on a remote Greek island, eating stranded men.
