"You're the Inspiration" is a song written by Peter Cetera and David Foster for the group Chicago and recorded for their album "Chicago 17" (1984), with Cetera singing lead vocals. The third single released from that album, it reached number 3 on the US "Billboard" Hot 100 chart in January 1985, and also climbed to the top position on the adult contemporary chart at the same time. Peter Cetera re-recorded the song for his 1997 solo album "". That same year he also recorded a single version with the vocal R&B group, Az Yet.
